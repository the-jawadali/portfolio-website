My Journey

The Scholars Programme

As part of the Scholars Programme, I had the opportunity to engage in university-style learning, supported by a PhD-level tutor throughout the term. The programme involved small group tutorials, in-school sessions, and trips to highly selective universities, providing a valuable insight into the demands of higher education. I completed a dissertation on Amino Acids, achieving a 2:1 grade. The project involved extensive research into the biochemical properties and functions of amino acids, allowing me to develop strong analytical, research, and writing skills. This experience not only deepened my understanding of biochemistry but also strengthened my ability to work independently, manage a long-term research project, and engage critically in academic discussions. It provided me with valuable skills in self-directed learning and problem-solving, which I continue to apply in my personal and professional growth.
